# Sales Performance Analysis & Dashboard (2025)

## 📊 Project Overview
This repository contains a comprehensive data analysis and Excel dashboard created for the Data Analyst Assignment. The dataset covers 2500+ sales records with details on products, customers, regions, discounts, and payment methods from January 2025 to December 2025.

---

## 🛠️ Key Steps Performed

### 1. Data Cleaning & Validation
* Identified and removed/handled duplicate records.
* Verified and handled missing values across key fields (`State`, `Payment_Mode`, `Customer_Name`).
* Checked data types and trimmed leading/trailing whitespace.
* Validated core calculations: `Sales = Unit Price × Quantity × (1 - Discount)` and `Profit = Sales - Cost`.

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis & Key Insights
* **Overall Metrics:** Calculated Total Sales, Total Cost, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, Total Orders, and Average Order Value.
* **Product Performance:** Identified top-performing product categories, highest-profit products, and products generating negative profits.
* **Regional Breakdown:** Analyzed sales contribution and profit margins across different regions and states.
* **Customer & Payment Analysis:** Tracked top customers, repeat order rates, and preferred payment methods.
* **Discount Impact:** Assessed how varying discount levels affect net profitability.

### 3. Interactive Excel Dashboard
* **KPI Cards:** Displaying Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, and Overall Margin %.
* **Visual Charts:** Monthly Sales Trend, Regional & Category Profitability, Top Products.
* **Slicers:** Interactive filtering by Region, Product Category, and Payment Method.
